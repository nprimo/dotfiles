---
name: grilling
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or asks to grill a plan.
---

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of the plan until you reach shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For every question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask one question at a time and wait for feedback before continuing. Multiple questions at once bewilder.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead of asking it.
